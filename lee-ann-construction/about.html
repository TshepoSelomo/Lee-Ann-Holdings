About us

We finish what we start.

Lee Ann Construction delivers building and civil works with disciplined project management — clear programmes, honest costing and safe sites. From a home renovation to a commercial build, we finish what we start.

As part of the group, our sites draw on in-house supply, transport and gas & aircon capability, which shortens lead times and removes coordination risk. Clients get the personal service of a specialist firm with the backing of an established holdings company.

We take on work we can stand behind, we price honestly, and we finish what we start.

Our approach

Consultation, planning and design, construction under quality and safety controls, then a clean handover.

Our promise

Safe sites, quality materials, honest pricing and delivery you can plan around.
