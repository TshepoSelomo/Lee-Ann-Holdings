About us

Built to move what others depend on — properly.

Lee Ann Transportation is the dedicated logistics and fleet division of Lee Ann Holdings. We move freight and equipment for businesses that cannot afford to miss a delivery window — planning routes, managing fleets and coordinating drivers so cargo arrives when we said it would.

From a single parcel to full-load freight, we focus on getting goods where they need to be: safely, efficiently and on schedule. Because we sit inside a group with procurement and construction capability, we can also move materials and plant as part of a wider project.

We take on work we can stand behind, we price honestly, and we finish what we start.

Our approach

Clear scoping, honest pricing and disciplined scheduling on every job — a single load or a recurring contract.

Our promise

Safe vehicles, vetted drivers, goods-in-transit cover and delivery you can plan around.
