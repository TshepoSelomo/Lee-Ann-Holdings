About us

Installed to standard. Certified on completion.

Lee Ann Gas & Aircon supplies, installs and services air conditioning, ventilation, refrigeration and gas systems for homes and businesses — with compliant, certified work on every installation.

We also run preventative maintenance contracts so systems are serviced before they fail, not after. Our technicians combine specialist HVAC expertise with the discipline and governance of a diversified group.

We take on work we can stand behind, we price honestly, and we finish what we start.

Our approach

Consult, quote clearly, install to standard, then test, hand over and certify.

Our promise

Registered gas work, manufacturer-standard installations and no hidden costs.
