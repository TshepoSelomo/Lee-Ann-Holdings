Services

What we do

Services & capabilities

Air conditioning supply & install

Split, cassette and ducted systems for homes, offices and retail — correctly sized and cleanly installed.

  • Split and multi-split systems
  • Ducted and central air conditioning
  • Load assessment and sizing

Servicing & maintenance

Planned servicing, deep cleaning and repairs for aircon, ventilation and refrigeration equipment.

  • Scheduled service plans
  • Filter cleaning and gas top-ups
  • Fault diagnosis and repair

Gas installations

LPG installations for heating, cooking and backup — installed by qualified technicians to regulation.

  • LPG installation and connection
  • Leak detection and testing
  • Stoves, heaters and geysers

Compliance certification

Certificates of conformity for gas installations, plus inspections for insurance and property sales.

Refrigeration

Commercial cold rooms, display fridges and refrigeration installation, repair and maintenance.

Energy assessments

HVAC energy audits and recommendations to reduce running costs and improve efficiency.

Emergency callouts

Rapid response when systems fail — keeping homes comfortable and businesses running.

Maintenance contracts

Preventative servicing so equipment is looked after before it fails, not after.
