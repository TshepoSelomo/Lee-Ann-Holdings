Spend & cost analysis

How we work

A clear, four-step methodology

1. Consult

We assess your needs, specifications, budget and timelines to define a clear procurement brief.

2. Source

We identify, benchmark and qualify suppliers, then negotiate price, quality and lead time.

3. Fulfil

Orders are placed, tracked and managed through to delivery, with quality checks throughout.

4. Report

You receive transparent reporting on spend, performance and savings to inform the next cycle.
