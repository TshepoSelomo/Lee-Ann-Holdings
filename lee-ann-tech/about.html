About us

Technology with a job to do — not a story to tell.

Lee Ann Tech is the technology division of Lee Ann Holdings. We design, install and support the systems a business runs on — networks, hardware, software and the people who keep them working.

We take on work we can stand behind, we price honestly, and we finish what we start.

Our approach

Clear scoping, honest pricing and iterative delivery so you always know where the work stands.

Our promise

Secure, scalable systems with support that stays on after go-live — not a hand-off into silence.
